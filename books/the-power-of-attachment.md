## Core Thesis
*The Power of Attachment* argues that the early attachment wounds we develop in childhood—manifesting as avoidant, ambivalent, or disorganized relational patterns—deeply shape how we connect, handle conflict, and regulate our nervous systems as adults, yet they are not permanent. Through somatic awareness, corrective relational experiences, and conscious self-regulation, anyone can rewire insecure patterns and cultivate "earned secure attachment" to build healthier, more resilient relationships.
