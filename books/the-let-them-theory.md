## Core Thesis
*The Let Them Theory* argues that trying to control other people’s opinions, moods, or behavior drains your energy and hands away your peace of mind. By consciously practicing "Let Them" (allowing others to act and think as they choose without resistance) followed by "Let Me" (taking full responsibility for your own boundaries, decisions, and responses), you reclaim emotional autonomy and focus your energy on what is actually within your control.

## Summary
### Key Idea 1: Stop wasting your life on things you can't control
- Humans have a hardwired need for control to feel safe. The problem is the fixation and directing of energy towards controlling things outside of your influence. 

- In trying to control everything, battling to control situations, worrying about what people say, think or do, one have created unnecessary stress tension and friction to self, because one will never be able to control or change another person. 

- The Let Them Theory is a framework for freedom that consists of two parts: "Let Them" and "Let Me." It's about releasing yourself from the burden of managing other people (Let Them) whilst taking responsibility for your own life (Let Me).

- The theory isn't about superiority but balance - giving others space to live their lives whilst giving yourself the same grace. Your happiness depends on your actions, not others' behaviour, and true empowerment comes from focusing your energy on what you can actually control.

### Key Idea 2: Emotion is a body response to stress, you can reset and control it
- Other people will bother you, annoy you, or stress you out. When you allow someone else’s behavior to stress you out, you let it dictate your mood, waste your time and energy, lose your focus and motivation, when you could be focused and feel happier.

- Your body’s stress response is automatic. You will feel anger, frustration, agitation. You can’t control the emotions that rise up inside of you. But instead of reacting and doing something you will regret, you can reset your stress response so you can be in control of your emotions.

- To reset our brain back to normal function, say Let Them happen, take a pause, then Let Me do something about it. By saying "Let Them", you acknowledge that you cannot control this situation that is stressing you out, not allowing it to spiral into other areas of your life. By saying "Let Me", it empowers you to determine your stressors and for how long,  before intentionally choose how to respond

- You can't change what just happened but you can do something about it. Let me stay engaged and vocal on the issues I care about and create the change you want to see, 

- The let them theory doesn't mean avoiding difficult conversations or staying silent. It doesn't mean letting people walk all over you. Nor does it mean you have to pick up after people. You get to choose what impacts you and to what extent. You get to choose what you participate in and what you don't. You get to choose when a job or relationship or issue is worth fighting for, and when its time to leave. You get to choose and that is why you are always in control of what happens.

### Key Idea 3: Fearing other people's opinion is draining
- You move through life with other people's opinions as your roadmap, forced to anticipate what other people opinions, afraid to look bad if people see the real you; it makes you overthink, procrastinate, self-doubt and self-reject your choices, paralyses you with perfectionism, drains your time and energy.

- Even if you please everyone, it won’t guarantee that other people will think a positive thought, and you will feel even more resentful, depleted and feeling like nothing you do was ever good enough.

- Whatever it is that you plan to do with your life, other people are going to have an opinion about it; allowing your fear of people's opinion stop you from doing what you want to do; Let them think what they want, Let Me live my life in a way that makes me proud and aligned with my values. Do not allow other people's opinion ruin me from living my life

- With family, it is harder to Let Them because there is little distance from them. They know you the longest, and want more for you and for you to be happier, but are more blunt, pushy and can very easily cross the line from caring to controlling. 

- Being part of the family system, any changes you make will send either positive or negative waves through the entire system.; Let them disagree with the way you are living your life to allow acceptance and understanding, try to understand their perspectives and be understanding and compassionate to deepen the connection.

### Key Idea 4: Dealing with someone else's emotional reaction
- Some adults throw tantrums because they were not taught to process human emotions in a healthy way growing up; By allowing their emotional immaturity to drive your decisions. you say yes when you really want to say no. This is why it’s hard for you to set boundaries. This is why you walk on eggshells when certain people are in a bad mood

- It feels easier to give in, but in the long run, you lose a crucial piece of yourself. When every interaction leaves you emotionally exhausted, ask yourself this: Why are you always the one who has to adjust? Why do you take make someone happy — at the expense of your own?

- Understand that emotions are just a burst of chemicals in your brain that rises up and falls quickly if you don't react to them, so let them rise and fall without doing anything. Even if other's emotions affect you, you can always choose how to response.

- It is not your responsibility to manage or fix emotionally immature adults. Just let them have the space to feel their emotions. Your responsibility is to do what is right for you and protect yourself, from their emotional spiral.

- The right logical decision often feels wrong when you know it will cause emotional pain; you'd rather inflict pain on yourself instead of making a decision that you know is right for you but painful for other people to accept; Let Them react to pain, instead of avoiding how your decision will impact them emotionally.

### Key Idea 5: Comparison can lead to resentment and inferiority
- It is human nature to compare yourself to other people. The problem is what you are doing with the comparison that matters; you feel that you are not good enough after comparing with other people and attributes you think are better than yours, you become your biggest obstacle. The focus now becomes on how unfair life seems to you. 

- You will feel stuck, hopeless and perpetually behind, leading to procrastination, self-criticism, low self-esteem. You do not see how much better off you are than majority of people around the world.

- Accept that life isn't fair and stop obsessing over what other people have or what they achieved. There is nothing you can do to change it, so learn to allow it. Let Them have their success. No one else's wins are your losses.

- You can instead focus your energy to figure out how to work with what you already have, how to learn from other's successes and work towards what you want in life. If you want something, it is your responsibility to work for it.

- Jealousy can be perceived as an invitation from your future self to show you what is possible (and not to feel inferior). Let me be inspired by what others did and turn it into action.

### Key Idea 6: Accept that friendships can drift
- When you were little, you were a part of this big group of people experiencing life together, planned by parents and society. As everyone scatters from theirs 20s, the expectations of friendship leads to disconnection, isolation and uncertainty in approaching new or existing friendships.

- Adult friendships drift away because one or more of these factors are missing: proximity (distance), timing (life chapter), energy (chemistry); We obsess over what is wrong and blaming others when friendship drift.

- Accept that friendships come and go; it is not personal. Take responsibility for understanding and processing your emotions in a healthy way. 

- Friendships are not a tit for tat. Do not keep score. Reach out to people because you want to. But don’t expect a response. How quickly or how often someone responds is not a sign of how much they care about you. It’s more likely an indication of how overwhelmed they may be by the chapter of life they are in. Assume good intent, Let them remain friends, cheer for them and wish them the best.

- Let me be the first to introduce myself, compliment people everywhere I go. Be curious. Smile and say hello to anyone I pass. Do this without expectations. It might take time, but trust that the right people will find their way to you.

- Look for groups that interest you. When you click with someone, take it out of the class. Reach out to people you have been hanging out with to go for events that are interesting

### Key Idea 7: People change from within, not by making them
- Wanting someone you love to change for the better is a good thing. But you can't change someone by pressuring them. It challenges their control over their own lives. It creates more tension, resentment and distance in your relationship with that person. 

- We feel frustration when people do not change because we judge, not accept, nor thinking about how hard the steps are for the person to change. The situation requires compassion and acceptance, not contempt when they are already feeling pressurised. 

- Change is hard for anyone because everyone thinks bad outcomes will not happen to them.  We are wired to move toward what feels good right now and to move away from what feels hard at the moment. We don't feel motivated to change because it does not feel good.

- Stop pressuring them and Let Them be. Let Them be on their own timeline, Let Them fail, Let Them give up, Let Them feel the consequences of their inaction. Let them motivate themselves to figure things out and change. Change comes from within.

- People are highly influenced and inspired by people around them. If they see something working positively for someone else, they feel interested in it too. So we can try to influence others to change by modelling the behaviour we want to see in others. Make it look fun and easy, but also understand it takes time for influence to take effect.

- How to (try to) influence someone, with the 5 Whys method (to prep) and the ABC loop (to talk about it)

5 Whys Method: Find the deeply personal reason why the behaviour stresses you so much that led to your need to control, by asking 
`Why does this person's behaviour bother me so much?`  five times

ABC Loop:
Apologise, then ask open-ended questions
```
"I want to apologise for judging and pressuring you, and I realised I never asked you how you feel* about you xxxx"
"So it sounds like ..."
"What makes you feel okay about it?"
"Do you feel like it's going to take too much work to change?"
"What feels hard about it?"
"So I hear you saying that you don't need me to do anything?"
"Can you tell me a little bit abut how long you have felt that way?"
"Have you thought about what You might want to do about it?"
```

- Back off and stop pressuring them. Let Them be. Space allows them to come to the idea for themselves, Time allows the tension to transform into motivation.

- Celebrate any progress you see immediately after you see them doing something about their behaviour. It makes them feel good

### Key Idea 8: People who are struggling must heal themselves
- People who are struggling tend to hide it from others. They feel like a burden to others so they don't ask for help or open up about what is happening. 

- As much as you may want to make someone's pain go away, you cannot make them heal. The more you try to rescue someone from their problems, the more likely they will continue to drown in them. 

- Allowing someone to face the natural consequences of their actions is a necessary part of healing. This means not enabling their behaviours, covering up for them, helping them avoid their issues, solving their problems for them; Enablement doesn't foster independence, hinder their healing and prolong their suffering. 

- People need kindness and support as they struggle. it means giving them the time, space, support and tools to do it themselves. Let Me validate theirs feelings, Let Me separate my emotions from theirs, Let Me comfort them, Let Me support them by assuring them that they have the ability to face their issues. Let Me create an environment that helps them to heal better, without expectations - they are often so overwhelmed they don't have the energy to respond back to you.

- Accept that not everyone is ready face their issues and heal. People need to want to get better for themselves, and that only happens when we Let Them struggle instead of avoiding the problem. Let Them hit rock bottom. If people are not ready to heal, Let Me stop supporting them and Let Me stop lying about what is happening.

### Key Idea 9: A healthy relationship is mutual
- Dating is hard because everybody is so scared to be alone, so desperate to find somebody, They chase love, and end up compromising on their values, standards, let others dictate their moods and affect their self-esteem.

- We convince ourselves that someone likes us, when we like them but also feel confused whether they like us back. We justify their bad behaviour and create fantasies in our heads to avoid breaking up. We chase the potential and ignore reality, hoping they will fall for us if we keep initiating. We change ourselves to "make it work" to chase something that isn't the right fit.

- People's behaviour tells you exactly where you stand in their life. You are either a priority or you are not. Let Them reveal who they truly are through their behaviour. Let Them ghost me. Let Me be honest to myself and admit things are going nowhere. Let Me remind myself that everything in a healthy relationship is mutual. Let Me move on.

- Dating is not just to find "the one", but it helps you learn more about yourself and what kind of behaviour you will not accept, how you want to be treated, and what kind of person you truly want to be in a relationship with. Dating becomes a process of elimination; You are looking for someone who is capable of helping you become your best self and co-creating a beautiful life together.

### Key Idea 10: Being honest about what you want in a relationship
- If you are always picking the "wrong" people, you might be attracted to people who you think you can win over. You live in the potential of what could be and not the reality of what it is. You need to be single and figure out how to be happy by yourself else you will end up bringing it to the next relationship, preventing your healing. 

- When you wonder if you are on the same page about what you want in your relationship. It's time for a honest conversation. Never feel bad about asking for what you deserve, never hint about something as important as a commitment. You’re seeking clarification because you have gotten to a point where you know that if this isn't going anywhere, it's no longer worth your time.

```
I value my time and energy, and I don’t want to put time and energy into spending time with someone if it’s not going to go to the next level. 

And I’ve reached that point with you. It’s been really fun. I love spending time with you. But I only want to invest more time and energy if we’re going to go to the next level. And if you don’t see the same thing, this has been great. But I just know myself and I need to choose to invest the time that I have with people who want the same things that I want
```

- Sometimes people you choose aren't going to choose you back. If they tell you that this is all they will commit to you, Let Them. Ask yourself if this is what you are willing to accept, why do you want to be with somebody who won't commit to you or want the same things you want? 

- Saying no to the wrong situations is how you find the right person for yourself. Let Me end a relationship with someone who won’t commit. Let Me trust this is another step in the direction of choosing the love I deserve. Let Me stop chasing the potential of this and see the reality. Let Me believe that I just took one step closer to the right person. Let Me take my power back, because the love of my life is right around the corner

### Key Idea 11: Ending a relationship opens up your life to new ones
- Every relationship that worked has had two important things present:
  - they both wanted the relationship to work, and they were both willing to do the work to make it better
  - the issues that created problems did not require either person to give up their dreams or compromise their values

- Anytime you find yourself questioning whether or not this is the right relationship for you, ask yourself: Can you accept this person exactly as they are, and exactly where they are, and still love them? Ask yourself: Do you want them to be just like you, or is it that one of your fundamental needs in the relationship is not being met? Because the reality of it is, they may never change (unless they want to). So before you spend time overthinking, have the conversation (using the ABC loop), then Let Them be.

- If after six months and the person hasn't changed or tried to, assume they aren't going to. It is not a priority of theirs or just are just incapable of changing. Let Them. Let Me stop fixing, Let Me accept it, and decide if this is a deal breaker (D) for me. Ask yourself: Could you be with this person for the rest of your life if they never, ever change? If yes, End the complains. If no, End the relationship because you can't accept the person and love them as they are; this is about you, not the other person .

- Ending a relationship is a very personal and difficult choice, especially when you wish it would work. It meant choosing to believe in that deep intuition inside of you that something about this just doesn't quite fit. Let Them go, even if it is not easy.

- When you go through heartbreak, it hurts so deeply because everything about them is intertwined in your nervous system and your experience of life. There is no avoiding it. You must unlearn what it was like to be with them and learn how to live life again without them. Research says it takes about three months to grieve a break up. But it will get better eventually. Let Me grieve, Let Me resist reaching out, Let Me feel depressed.

- Some recommendations to settling your nervous system and moving through a breakup - it is not time that heals, but what you do with that time that matters.
  - Remove all environment triggers - take every visible reminder and get them out of sight.
  - Give your bedroom a small makeover - if you spend a lot of time together in your bedroom, a refresh helps signal that a new chapter is beginning.
  - Fill your calendar - distracting yourself from thinking about them by filling your calendar with events
  - Pick a challenge you have always wanted to do - Do something for yourself that you are proud of. 
  - Keep asking yourself, how would you spend your nights and weekends while you are single, if you knew the love of your life is around the corner

- Let Me priortize my own happiness, Let Me pursue my dreams with passion, Let Me set boundaries that protect my peace, Let Me choose relationships that uplift and inspire me. Let Me love myself enough to walk away when it no longer works.
